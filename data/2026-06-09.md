### [1] [Crayotter: Traceable Multi-Agent Workflows for Long-Form Video Editing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07636)

Crayotter organizes production into three phases: coverage-aware material preparation, artifact-based editing research, and tool-grounded timeline execution.
