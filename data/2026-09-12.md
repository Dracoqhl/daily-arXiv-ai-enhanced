### [19] [Artificial Id: Drive and Persistent Alignment in Agentic AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11911)

Main category: cs.AI

TL;DR: Agentic AI is moving from bounded task execution toward systems that retain consequential state, continue operating and adapt across task boundaries.
